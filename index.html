BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A Few Helpful Answers

How do I book an appointment?+

Choose your services and preferred date and time, then send your request on WhatsApp. The salon will confirm availability and your appointment.

Does the price depend on my hair length?+

Several services have separate prices for short, medium and long hair. Choose the matching option in the service menu. The salon will confirm the final price on WhatsApp.

Not sure which treatment to choose?+

Message the salon on WhatsApp with your hair concerns and the result you would like. Ask for advice before choosing a treatment.

Where are you, and when are you open?+

Find us on 22nd Street, Al Barsha 1, Dubai. We are open every day from 10 AM to 10 PM. Use Get Directions below to plan your visit.
